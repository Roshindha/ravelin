# Troop stats

The battlefield uses four troop archetypes with distinct combat roles. Costs are paired so a 200-resource budget encourages meaningful trade-offs rather than a single dominant pick.

| Name | Cost | HP | Damage | Speed | Range | Role | Target preference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| swarm | 10 | 18 | 8 | 1.5 | 1.2 | cheap skirmisher | building > turret > wall |
| ranger | 20 | 34 | 12 | 1.0 | 5.0 | ranged attacker | turret > building > wall |
| tank | 30 | 90 | 24 | 0.7 | 1.4 | frontliner | wall > building > turret |
| splash | 40 | 42 | 30 | 0.6 | 2.5 | wall-breaker | wall > building > turret |

The budget of 200 allows cheap swarm rushes, selective ranged pressure, armor-heavy pushes, and splash-heavy wall breaking. No single template dominates every base layout.
